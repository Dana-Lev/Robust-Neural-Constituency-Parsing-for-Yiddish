vocabulary injection (FOCUS) + continued pre-training (DAPT) activates new

addressed \emph{dynamically} and parameter-efficiently -- BPE-dropout-style
subword regularization \citep{provilkov-etal-2020-bpe} and language adapters
\citep{pfeiffer-etal-2020-mad} -- without touching the vocabulary or
pre-training? And, as a control demanded by the pace of the field: do frontier
LLMs already parse historical Yiddish, making a custom parser unnecessary?
(4) Contributions bullet list.}

\todo{The ``tokenization tax'': XLM-R \citep{conneau-etal-2020-unsupervised}

(a) \emph{static}: vocabulary injection with FOCUS

\citep{provilkov-etal-2020-bpe} for BPE and Kudo sampling
\citep{kudo-2018-subword} for Unigram tokenizers like XLM-R's. Point out the
gap: (b) has not been tested for low-resource constituency parsing with frozen
encoders.}
